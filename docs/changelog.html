3.6.0STABLE RELEASE30 October 2026

Glass Edition

A major refinement of the desktop experience, with a more consistent, readable, and focused interface.

  • Graphite and emerald visual system with glass surfaces, subtle depth, and smoother transitions.
  • Assistant typography and Material Symbols Rounded icons throughout the interface.
  • Improved conversation management: rename, duplicate, pin, favorites, folders, tags, bookmarks, and restore points.
  • Conversation export in Markdown, HTML, and JSON formats.
  • Improved readability, responsive layouts, focus states, and reduced-motion support.
